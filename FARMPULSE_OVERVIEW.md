# FarmPulse Malawi: What Our System Does

> **In simple terms:** FarmPulse Malawi is an all-in-one digital assistant for farmers. It removes the guesswork from farming by telling farmers **what to plant**, **when it will rain**, and **where to sell their harvest for the highest profit**.

---

## 1. Why Was FarmPulse Built?

Most smallholder farmers in Malawi face three big challenges every season:

1. **Unpredictable Weather:** Rains start late, dry spells kill young crops, or floods wash away fertilizer.
2. **Unfair Market Prices:** Middlemen ("vendors") buy crops very cheaply at the farmgate because farmers do not know the actual city market prices.
3. **Expensive Inputs:** Fertilizer and certified seeds are costly. When farmers apply the wrong amount, they lose money.

**FarmPulse was built to solve these problems by putting real-time data and actionable advice directly into the hands of farmers and agricultural officers.**

---

## 2. What Does the System Actually Do?

FarmPulse is organized into five easy-to-use parts:

### 1. The Smart Crop Planner (Making Farming Profitable)
* **What it does:** A farmer enters their district, soil type, and land size (e.g., 2 acres in Lilongwe).
* **The output:** The system instantly calculates:
  * Exactly how many bags of certified seed and fertilizer (NPK and Urea) to buy.
  * How much field labour is required.
  * The total cost to grow the crop.
  * Expected harvest yield and projected profit in Malawian Kwacha (MWK).
* **Benefit:** Farmers know their costs and expected profits *before* spending a single Kwacha.

---

### 2. Live Weather Intelligence (Protecting Crops from Drought)
* **What it does:** FarmPulse connects directly to weather stations across Malawi (such as Chitedze, Kasungu, Salima, and Bvumbwe).
* **What the farmer sees:**
  * **14-Day Live Forecast:** Accurate daily temperatures and rainfall chances.
  * **Dry-Spell Warnings:** Alerts farmers if a dry period of 7 days or more is coming, so they can hold off on applying top-dressing fertilizer.
  * **Planting Window Detector:** Confirms when enough cumulative rain (at least 25 mm) has fallen to safely plant seeds without them drying out.
* **Benefit:** Prevents lost seeds and wasted fertilizer caused by planting too early.

---

### 3. Market Intelligence & Smart Selling (Beating the Middlemen)
* **What it does:** FarmPulse tracks daily crop prices across major trading hubs in Malawi (including Mitundu, Mchesi, Limbe, Lunzu, and Chinkhoma).
* **Three Clear Price Levels:**
  * **Farmgate Price (Amber):** What vendors pay directly in the village.
  * **Wholesale Price (Green):** What commercial buyers and millers pay for bulk 50 kg bags.
  * **Retail Price (Blue):** What consumers pay per kilogram in town.
* **Smart Selling Tools:**
  * **Where to Sell (Arbitrage):** The system calculates transport costs to show if renting a truck to sell in a city market (like Limbe or Mchesi) will yield more profit than selling at the village gate.
  * **Sell Now vs. Store Later:** Compares today's price with the 30-day projected price. If prices are expected to rise significantly, it advises farmers to store grain in sealed hermetic (PICS) bags instead of selling cheaply during harvest glut.
* **Benefit:** Farmers earn up to 30%–50% more money for the exact same harvest.

---

### 4. The Scenario Simulator (A "Flight Simulator" for Farming)
* **What it does:** Lets farmers test "what-if" situations before planting:
  * *"What if there is a 3-week drought during flowering?"*
  * *"What if fertilizer prices jump by 25%?"*
  * *"What if maize market prices crash?"*
* **Benefit:** Helps farmers choose resilient crops (such as drought-tolerant groundnuts, soybeans, or cassava) so they are protected no matter what happens.

---

### 5. The Innovation Lab (Built for Every Malawian Farmer)
Not all farmers have smartphones or internet access. FarmPulse includes specialized tools to reach everyone:

* **USSD Phone Menu (`*384#`):** Works on simple, basic "button phones" with no internet. Farmers can dial a short code to check local market prices and weather updates via SMS/text.
* **Satellite Field Twin:** Uses space satellite imagery (Sentinel-2) to check crop health and moisture levels from above. It spots stressed patches in a field before pests or diseases spread.
* **Parametric Weather Insurance:** Automatically calculates insurance protection for crops. If satellite data detects extreme drought during the growing season, a mobile-money payout (Airtel Money / TNM Mpamba) is triggered automatically—no paperwork or claim forms required.
* **Mlimi AI Assistant:** An artificial intelligence advisor that speaks both **Chichewa** and **English**. Farmers can ask questions like *"Why are my maize leaves turning yellow?"* or *"Kodi ndingadzale liti mtedza?"* and receive immediate agronomic advice.

---

## 3. Who Uses FarmPulse?

* **Smallholder Farmers:** To plan their season, know market prices, and protect their crops.
* **Farmer Cooperatives & Clubs:** To aggregate produce, negotiate wholesale contracts, and rent shared transport.
* **Lead Farmers & Government Extension Workers (AEDOs):** To advise entire villages using scientific, verified data.
* **Agribusinesses & Off-takers:** To find where crops are being harvested and plan purchases.
* **Financial Institutions & Insurers:** To offer fair loans and micro-insurance based on real data.

## 4. Summary: The Value in One Sentence

> **FarmPulse transforms traditional Malawian smallholder farming into a planned, data-driven, and profitable business by connecting field-level planning with live weather forecasts and real-time market prices.**
