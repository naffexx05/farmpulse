Copernicus Sentinel-2 Integration

See Your Field from 786 km in Space Before Stress Kills Your Crop

Every 5 days, the European Space Agency’s Sentinel-2 constellation orbits over Malawi. FarmPulse parses optical (Band 4) and Near-Infrared (Band 8) reflectance to map chlorophyll vigor (NDVI) and topsoil moisture across every registered boundary.

1
Chlorophyll Canopy Index (NDVI): Detect nitrogen deficiency or Fall Armyworm attacks 2 weeks before yellowing is visible to the naked eye.
2
Moisture Anomaly Tracking: High-resolution soil moisture maps alert farmers to localized dry-spell hot-spots.
3
Sub-Hectare Farm Geometry: Polygons accurate down to 10-meter pixel resolution tailored for Malawian fragmented plots.

End-to-End Retention System

From First Seed to Final Harvest Report

FarmPulse does not abandon the farmer after generating a plan. The Season Journey walks hand-in-hand through all 6 stages of production with weekly check-ins and financial bookkeeping.

STAGE 01 Month 0

1. Plan Adoption

Farmer activates their optimized strategy (e.g. Balanced 2.5 ha). Budget is locked, benchmarks set, and target yields registered.

STAGE 02 Weeks 1 - 2

2. Land Prep & Inputs

Record tractor hire, seed purchases, and basal D-Compound applications in the digital ledger. DCCMS confirms planting moisture window.

STAGE 03 Weeks 3 - 4

3. Planting & Germination

Farmer logs emergence rate. Smart advisory engine alerts on gap-filling if emergence drops below 85% to protect projected yields.

STAGE 04 Weeks 5 - 12

4. Growth Watch & Advisories

Weekly 60-second check-ins: pest pressure, top-dress Urea timing at knee-height, and satellite chlorophyll verification.

STAGE 05 Weeks 16 - 20

5. Harvest Recording

Log actual 50kg bags harvested, market sale prices, and buyer contacts. Income automatically credited against input expenses.

STAGE 06 Annual Review

6. Season Report & Accuracy

System calculates Plan Accuracy Score (e.g. 92%), net ROI, and generates actionable improvements for the upcoming 2026/27 cycle.
