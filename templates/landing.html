System Architecture

Three Foundational Intelligence Pillars

1. Climate Intelligence

Integrates DCCMS weather stations and 10-year historical rainfall curves to evaluate dry-spell probability, heat stress windows, and optimal planting dates before capital is spent.

2. Market Intelligence

Monitors commodity prices across 6 trading hubs (Lilongwe, Mzuzu, Limbe, Luchenza). Features a transport-cost calculator that proves whether shipping grain yields higher net profit.

3. Enterprise Crop Planning

Algorithms balance available land, capital, and labor against certified input costs. Generates Food Security, High-Margin, and Climate-Resilient strategies with exact breakeven metrics.
