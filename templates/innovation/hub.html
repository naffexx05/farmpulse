The FarmPulse Innovation Showcase

Four breakthrough technological advancements solving the last-mile accessibility, earth observation, climate de-risking, and contextual agronomic AI challenges across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Flagship 1

Satellite Field Twin & Earth Observation

Replaces coarse district averages with 10-meter cadastral polygon tracking via Sentinel-2 and radar (SAR). Calculates real-time NDVI canopy photosynthetic vigor and 0–5 cm dielectric soil moisture independent of cloud cover.

Flagship 2

Zero-Data 2G USSD (*384*77#) Terminal

Overcomes the smartphone divide for the 75%+ of rural smallholders who only possess basic $10 button phones. Fully functioning USSD state machine delivering live AMIS prices, dry-spell advisories, and break-even calculations without internet data.

Flagship 3

Parametric Satellite Drought Shield

Replaces slow, subjective human adjusters with satellite rainfall index smart contracts. Automatically triggers immediate indemnity disbursements directly to Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba mobile wallets when dry spells exceed threshold during critical silking.

Flagship 4

Mlimi AI: Bilingual Agronomic Copilot

An autonomous agronomic AI available in full domestic Chichewa (*Chichewa cha Alimi*) and English. Dynamically ingests the farmer's soil types, field acreage, planted crops, and district dry-spell forecasts to output scientifically optimized fertilizer and bio-defense plans.
