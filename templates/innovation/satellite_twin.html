3 Proven Field Ingestion Pathways (Addressing the Last Mile)

How FarmPulse brings real smallholder fields onto satellite maps without requiring expensive survey equipment:

1. Mobile GPS Perimeter Walk

Farmer & AEDO Extension Mode

Agricultural Extension Development Officers (AEDOs) or farmers walk the field boundary with any GPS-enabled Android smartphone. The FarmPulse PWA records corner points offline in browser IndexedDB and syncs when connectivity returns.

2. Interactive Satellite Map Drawing

EPA Office & Hub Mode

At EPA offices or lead farmer hubs, farmers identify visible landmarks (hedgerows, access paths, ridges, drainage contours) on high-resolution 0.5-meter imagery, clicking to draw and snap their cadastral polygon within 60 seconds.

3. Cooperative & Ministry Bulk Import

Enterprise & FUM Mode

Direct ingestion of existing ESRI Shapefiles, KML, and GeoJSON cadastres from the Ministry of Agriculture Land Resources Department, NASFAM, or the Farmers Union of Malawi (FUM) to onboard entire cooperatives in batch.
