Heat Stress on Maize

Temperatures >32°C during anthesis (tasseling and silking) reduce pollen viability and seed set, leading to barren cobs.

Heat Stress on Legumes

Groundnuts and soybeans abort blossoms when maximum ambient temperature exceeds 34°C, limiting pod development.

Irrigation Mitigation

Farms with drip or furrow irrigation can moderate canopy microclimates during heat waves, mitigating yield loss by up to 35%.
